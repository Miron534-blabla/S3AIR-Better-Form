# Sonic 3 A.I.R. — Better Form (Remastered, Full Edition)

<img width="256" height="256" alt="Icon" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/514157ea-41ae-49f5-9854-3dce1f291c7f" />

**Author:** Miron_534

A comprehensive rebalance and visual overhaul mod for Super and Hyper forms in **Sonic 3 A.I.R.** Includes both the **Full Edition** (all characters) and **3 standalone modules** (Sonic only, Tails only, Knuckles only).

---

## 🌟 Global Features
* **Step-by-Step Transformation:** Option to transform into Super first, then into Hyper (configurable).
* **Custom Hyper Ring Cost:** Choose between 50, 75, or 100 rings required for Hyper transformation.
* **Custom Ring Drain:** Option to slow down Hyper ring drain to `0.5x`.
* **Doomsday Zone Form Chooser:** Select whether to play Doomsday Zone as Super or Hyper (both in normal play and Level Select).
* **Super/Hyper Stars for Everyone:** Sparkle effects are now available for Tails and Knuckles, not just Sonic.
* **Rebalanced Physics & Custom Sprites:** Super forms match standard Super physics, while Hyper forms are faster, stronger, and feature revamped sprites.

---

## ⚡ Character Buffs

### 🔵 Sonic
* Buffed Hyper jump height and movement speed.
* Customizable eye color (Green / Red) and transformation flash toggle.
### 🟠 Tails
* **Multi-Flight (Unlimited Flight):** Configurable for Always, Super & Hyper only, or Hyper only.
* **Vortex Air Dash:** Press `A` in mid-air to perform a swirling dash.
* **Break Walls:** Super & Hyper Tails can smash through breakable walls and obstacles.
* **Buffed Flickies:** Improved Flicky behavior, custom eye colors, and animation fixes.

### 🔴 Knuckles
* **Horizontal Gliding:** Hold `Up` while gliding as Super/Hyper to glide straight horizontally without losing altitude.
* **Hyper Glide Flight:** Hold `A` while gliding as Hyper Knuckles to gain altitude.
* **Hyper Air Dash:** Perform a directional mid-air dash just like Hyper Sonic, with customizable button bindings.

---

## 📥 Installation
1. Download the `.zip` archive from the **Releases** section on the right (choose either the Full Edition or an individual character version).
2. Place the `.zip` file into your Sonic 3 A.I.R. `mods` folder.
3. Enable the mod in the in-game **Mods** menu and customize the settings to your liking!
